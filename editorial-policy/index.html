Editorial Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Our Mission

De Image Magazine is committed to delivering accurate, timely, and meaningful journalism. Our goal is to inform, educate, and engage a global audience while maintaining the highest standards of editorial integrity.

2. Accuracy & Fact-Checking

We prioritize accuracy in all content we publish. Our editorial team verifies information through credible sources and cross-checks facts before publication.

  • Multiple-source verification where applicable
  • Use of credible and authoritative references
  • Continuous review of published content

3. Editorial Independence

Our editorial decisions are made independently of advertisers, sponsors, or external influences. We maintain a clear separation between editorial content and advertising.

4. Sponsored & Branded Content

Sponsored content is clearly labeled to ensure transparency. While such content may be created in collaboration with partners, it must meet our editorial standards and provide value to readers.

5. Corrections & Updates

If an error is identified, we correct it promptly and transparently. Updated content may include a note indicating changes or revisions.

6. Sources & Attribution

We credit sources appropriately and respect intellectual property. Quotes, data, and references are attributed to their original sources whenever possible.

7. Ethical Standards

We adhere to ethical journalism practices, including fairness, balance, and accountability.

  • No misinformation or misleading headlines
  • No undisclosed conflicts of interest
  • Respect for individuals’ rights and privacy

8. User Contributions

Guest submissions, comments, or contributions must align with our editorial standards. We reserve the right to edit or reject content that does not meet our guidelines.

Our Commitment

Trust is the foundation of our publication. We are committed to maintaining transparency, accountability, and integrity in everything we publish.

We don’t just publish content — we uphold standards that define credible journalism.

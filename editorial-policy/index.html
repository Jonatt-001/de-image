De Image Magazine · Newsroom Standards

Editorial Policy.

The principles, standards and safeguards that guide how De Image Magazine reports, verifies, corrects and publishes information.

Public newsroom standard Last updated: August 2026
On this page

01

Our Mission

De Image Magazine is committed to delivering accurate, timely, meaningful and responsibly reported journalism. Our goal is to inform, educate and engage our audience while maintaining strong standards of editorial integrity, accountability and public trust.

02

Accuracy & Fact-Checking

Accuracy is fundamental to our journalism. Reporters and editors are expected to verify material facts before publication and to distinguish confirmed information from allegations, analysis, opinion or developing reports.

  • Multiple-source verification where reasonably possible.
  • Use of credible, relevant and authoritative sources.
  • Direct review of names, dates, figures, quotations and claims.
  • Clear attribution when information comes from external sources.
  • Additional verification for sensitive or consequential claims.
03

Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions are made according to journalistic relevance, public interest and our standards. Advertisers, sponsors, commercial partners and other outside interests must not determine the substance or outcome of independent editorial coverage.

04

Sponsored & Branded Content

Sponsored, branded or commercially supported material is clearly identified so readers can distinguish it from independent editorial reporting. Commercial partnerships do not automatically imply editorial endorsement.

Transparency comes first: readers should always be able to understand whether they are reading independent editorial content or material produced with commercial support.
05

Corrections & Updates

When a material error is identified, we aim to correct it promptly and transparently. Depending on the nature of the change, published articles may include an update or correction note so readers can understand what was changed.

06

Sources & Attribution

We credit sources appropriately and respect intellectual property. Quotes, data, documents, research and other material originating outside De Image Magazine should be attributed accurately whenever possible. We do not present another party's work as our own.

07

Ethical Standards

Our newsroom seeks to report fairly, responsibly and with appropriate regard for the rights and dignity of individuals. Journalism should serve a legitimate public or editorial purpose and should not deliberately mislead readers.

  • No knowingly false or materially misleading reporting.
  • No deliberately deceptive headlines or presentation.
  • Conflicts of interest should be disclosed and appropriately managed.
  • Privacy should be respected except where overriding public interest applies.
  • Claims and allegations should be presented with appropriate context.
08

User Contributions

Guest submissions, comments, tips and other contributions may be reviewed, edited, moderated or rejected where necessary. Submission of material does not guarantee publication. Contributors remain responsible for the accuracy and legality of material they provide.

Our Commitment

Trust is earned through standards.

We do not expect readers to trust us simply because we publish. We aim to earn that trust through accuracy, transparency, accountability, responsible sourcing and a willingness to correct our work when we get something wrong.
